## Feature 2: three things

id: f2_eyebrow  (Eyebrow)
Three things, then the journal

id: f2_title  (Title)
Gratitude and the sit, done together.

id: f2_text  (Text)
Three lines before the day starts. Small is fine: the coffee, the light on the wall, a text from someone. A year later the app hands the line back to you on the same date. Below it, a plain journal with headings, lists, a checklist and photos, saved as you type.

id: f2_small  (Small line)
Tap a sun through the day to log your vibe. The thirty-day line is the seven-day average; the grid is every day you sat and wrote.

## About

id: a_title  (Title)
Anitya is the word for impermanence.

id: a_p1  (Paragraph 1)
It is the word you hear for ten days on a Vipassana course: everything arises and passes, so you sit, observe, and start again. Anitya Labs makes the few things that help a daily sit stick, starting with an app that shows the practice working in your own numbers.

id: a_p2  (Paragraph 2)
It began as one person's 4am routine, a ring, and four years of three-things lists kept in a journal. The app is that routine, tidied up so anyone can keep it.
