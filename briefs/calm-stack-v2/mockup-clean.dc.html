What's in it?
Morning Saffron · Usha. Serving size 1 capsule · 60 servings per container. Saffron extract (Crocus sativus L.) (flower) 88.5 mg. Daily Value not established. Other ingredients: rice flour, hypromellose (vegetable capsule).
Evening Magnesium · Sandhya. Serving size 2 capsules · 45 servings per container. Magnesium 183 mg (44% DV), from 1,667 mg magnesium glycinate. Other ingredients: hypromellose (capsule), magnesium stearate, silicon dioxide, rice flour.

Monday to Friday. Order by 11am Mountain Time on a weekday and it ships that day; later orders ship the next weekday. Our warehouse is closed for Thanksgiving on 26 and 27 November, so orders from those days ship on Monday 30 November. You get a DHL tracking number the day it leaves.

No. Morning Saffron is 60 mornings at one capsule. Evening Magnesium is 45 evenings at two capsules. The Practice and The Season give you more of both.
How long until I notice it?
Saffron is subtle, so give it a few weeks and keep tapping each morning. Looking back at your own taps is more reliable than trying to remember. You have 60 mornings to decide.
Why not just buy saffron threads?
You can. A pinch in hot water is a fine drink. A capsule gives you the same 88.5 mg of saffron extract every morning, so what you track stays the same.
Can I take it with my medication?
Ask your doctor or pharmacist before adding any supplement, especially if you take medication for mood or blood pressure.
